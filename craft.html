Why This Happens

It's usually not the technology. Not the developers. Not the framework.

It's the system.

Good engineers inside a poor engineering system eventually produce poor outcomes. Adding developers rarely fixes an organizational problem. Changing frameworks rarely fixes an engineering culture.

Complexity compounds — quietly, relentlessly — unless someone intentionally reduces it. That's the real reason growing companies slow down.
