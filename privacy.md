## What We Collect

- **Newsletter:** Your email address, and the page you subscribed from.
- **Bookings:** Your name, email address and whatever you tell us about your situation when you book a call or session.
- **Billing:** Payment details are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
- **Usage data:** Anonymous, aggregated analytics about how the site is used (pages visited, country). We do not track you across other websites.

## How We Use Your Information

- To run the call or engagement you booked
- To send the newsletter you subscribed to
- To improve the site based on aggregate usage

We do not sell your personal information.

## Third-Party Services

- **[Calendly](https://calendly.com/):** Scheduling for calls and sessions. See [Calendly's Privacy Notice](https://calendly.com/privacy).
- **[Stripe](https://stripe.com/):** Payment processing. See [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
- **[Resend](https://resend.com/):** Sends the newsletter and stores subscriber email addresses. See [Resend's Privacy Policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **[Fathom Analytics](https://usefathom.com/):** Privacy-focused, cookieless website analytics. See [Fathom's Privacy Policy](https://usefathom.com/privacy).
- **[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/):** Hosts the website. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Cookies and Local Storage

CTOConnect does not set tracking cookies. When you subscribe to the newsletter, the site stores a single flag in your browser so subscriber-only articles stay open on that device. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

## Data Retention

Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Booking and engagement records are kept for as long as needed for the engagement and for tax and accounting purposes. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

## Your Rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and you can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, email kyle@ctoconnect.io.
