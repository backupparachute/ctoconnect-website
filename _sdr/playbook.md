## Replies

- **"Can he just fix it / do the work?"** No. Not ticket work. Kyle gives you the decision and the plan. Your team does the build.
- **"Can I just Slack him questions?"** Not a Slack coach. If the calls keep coming, Fractional is a weekly partner.
- **"$600 is a lot."** Compare it to one wrong hire or one quarter on the wrong rewrite. Then ask: priority this quarter, or bad timing?
- **"We need ongoing help."** Send them to the Apply button on the same page.
- **"No thanks" / "not now".** Stop. No further follow-up.
