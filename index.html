Everyone Is Busy. The Roadmap Still Slips.

You hired more developers. You adopted AI. Individual engineers are faster than they have ever been. There is more code, more pull requests, more activity in every dashboard you look at.

And the dates keep moving.

More development activity does not mean more business throughput. A company can double its output and ship nothing customers notice. The fix is rarely "write code faster." Someone has to step back and find out why all that motion isn't turning into product.
